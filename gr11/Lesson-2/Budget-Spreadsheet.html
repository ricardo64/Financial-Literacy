Task 4: 1-Year University Budget Spreadsheet

Grade 11 Life Skills — Money Management & Financial Planning (2028)

Step 1: Choose Your Preferred Format

Select how you would like to open your 2-tab budget planning template:

📊 Download Microsoft Excel Saves .xlsx file directly to your device 🟢 Open in Google Sheets Creates a copy directly in your Google Drive

Step 2: How to Complete Your Budget

  1. Open the Template: Launch your copied file in Google Sheets or open the downloaded file in Microsoft Excel.
  2. Open Your Task 3 Screenshot: Have your Task 3 research summary screenshot open next to your spreadsheet window.
  3. Populate Tab 1 (Student Worksheet): Enter your researched costs into the payment amount boxes. Watch your 1-Year totals calculate automatically!
  4. Balance Your Budget: Adjust your funding/income sources until your Net Annual Position turns GREEN (Surplus).
💡 Need help or stuck on numbers? Click on Tab 2 (Worked Example) at the bottom of your spreadsheet to see a completed benchmark budget for a student studying in Germany!

Step 3: Toddle Submission Checklist

Before submitting your finished spreadsheet on Toddle, ensure you have checked:

  • All your Task 3 expense categories are transferred into Tab 1.
  • Your monthly vs. one-off payment frequencies are set correctly.
  • Your Net Position cell turns green, showing a positive annual balance.
  • You have saved or exported your completed spreadsheet and attached it to Toddle.
